General terms governing travel inquiries, booking requests, and third-party services.

1. Nature of Booking Inquiries

Submitting an inquiry through this website does not constitute a guaranteed booking or confirmed ticket reservation. All airfares, hotel room allocations, and tour dates are subject to real-time airline and property availability and final price confirmation at the time of payment.

2. Third-Party Airline and Hotel Policies

Worldwide Tours acts as an intermediary travel booking agent. All flights, accommodation stays, and third-party transport are subject to the specific terms, baggage policies, check-in conditions, and cancellation guidelines of the operating carrier or hotel property.

3. Passenger Responsibility & Travel Documents

It is the passenger's sole responsibility to ensure that passports have at least 6 months validity from travel date, that valid national identity cards (CNIC) are held for domestic Pakistan flights, and that all required transit and entry visas, health declarations, and entry permits are secured before departure.

4. Cancellations, Changes and Refunds

Cancellation fees, refundability, and date-change penalties are determined by the fare rules of the airline or hotel booked. Non-refundable promotional fares cannot be refunded after ticket issuance. Worldwide Tours applies standard administrative processing according to supplier regulations.
